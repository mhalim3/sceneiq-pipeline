# Source lists

Tiers start from Wikipedia's Perennial Sources list (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources/Perennial_sources, fetched 2026-10-13), where "generally reliable" is the top group, and add our own split. The rules live in `sceneiq/source_policy.py`.

- **A** = Wikipedia "generally reliable" AND film/entertainment is the outlet's core beat (a film fact is inside its area of expertise), plus official records and domain authorities (ASCAP, BMI, FilmLA, ASC, Criterion, .gov). (29 domains)
- **B** = Wikipedia "generally reliable" but a general-interest outlet; Wikipedia "no consensus" entertainment or reference outlets; sites where Wikipedia entries disagree; and trade outlets Wikipedia does not rate. (210 domains)
- **C** = other Wikipedia "no consensus", and "generally unreliable", "deprecated" or "blacklisted". Lead only, never support a fact. (494 domains)
- **Unknown** = not on Wikipedia's list or ours; the AI judge decides per claim. Video platforms (YouTube, Vimeo) are always left to the judge.
