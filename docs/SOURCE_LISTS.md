# Source lists

Tiers follow Wikipedia's Perennial Sources list (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources/Perennial_sources, fetched 2026-10-13).

- **A** = Wikipedia "generally reliable" (183 domains)
- **B** = Wikipedia "no consensus" (usable depending on context) (172 domains)
- **C** = Wikipedia "generally unreliable", "deprecated" or "blacklisted"; lead only, never support (393 domains)
- **Unknown** = not on Wikipedia's list and not in our local additions; the AI judge decides per claim.
- Video platforms (YouTube, Vimeo) are always left to the judge.
- Where a site has several Wikipedia entries that disagree (e.g. a culture section vs the rest), it is treated as B.
