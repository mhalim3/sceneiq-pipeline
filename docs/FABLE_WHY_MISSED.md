# Ask Fable: why did the research miss these?

Paste this into the SAME Fable chat that produced the Step Brothers research (so it can see its own work), or attach its fact list.

---

You researched facts for Step Brothers (2008). A separate pipeline (Gemini with Google Search) researched the same film, and the facts below ended up in our final list. Some of them were not in your list.

For each fact below:
1. Say whether your list already had it (yes / partly / no).
2. If no or partly, explain honestly why you missed it. Pick the closest reason and say more:
   a. You never searched for that topic.
   b. You searched but did not find a page that states it.
   c. You found a source but it was a tier you excluded (Tier C, YouTube, Wikipedia, IMDb), so you dropped it.
   d. You found it but judged it not interesting enough, or it matched a "do not show" rule.
   e. You found it but could not verify it with a verbatim passage.
   f. Something else (say what).
3. Name the single change to your instructions or search approach that would have caught it (a search you should have run, a rule that was too strict, a source type you should have allowed).

Then finish with:
- The three most common reasons across all facts.
- The five changes to the instructions that would recover the most missed facts, ranked, each in one sentence.
- Any fact below you now think is wrong or unsupported, and why.

FACTS IN OUR FINAL LIST:

1. Short: Actor Richard Jenkins (Dale's Dad) worked for co-star John C. Reilly's dad.
   Long: Actor Richard Jenkins (Dale's Dad) worked for co-star John C. Reilly's dad, driving a laundry truck. He met a 4-year-old Reilly in 1969.
2. Short: Jon Hamm was one of the finalists for the role of Derek (played by Adam Scott).
   Long: Jon Hamm was a finalist for Derek (Adam Scott's role). Scott won the part just before Hamm's breakout on 'Mad Men.'
3. Short: According to Will Ferrell, the prop testicles cost $10,000.
   Long: According to Will Ferrell, the prop testicles rubbed on the drum set cost $10,000. The director reportedly said $25,000.
4. Short: You can buy tickets to the real Catalina Wine Mixer.
   Long: Inspired by the movie, the Catalina Wine Mixer became a real event in 2015. It's now a three-day festival you can buy tickets to.
5. Short: Step Brothers started as a one-line pitch: 'Bunk Beds.'
   Long: Step Brothers grew from a one-line 'bunk beds' joke Will Ferrell and John C. Reilly made while working on Talladega Nights.
6. Short: Step Brothers shot so much film that Kodak sent champagne.
   Long: Thanks to nonstop improv, Step Brothers shot 1.5 million feet of film, and Kodak sent over champagne.
7. Short: John C. Reilly was abandoned by his friends before a real childhood gang fight.
   Long: The playground fight scene was based on John C. Reilly's childhood. He tried to start a gang fight, but his friends abandoned him alone.
8. Short: A cut 14-page SeaWorld trip from Step Brothers was reused in Anchorman 2.
   Long: After a fake intruder scare, Dale and Brennan asked to just go to SeaWorld. The 14-page run was cut, but Anchorman 2 has a SeaWorld scene.
9. Short: During the Catalina Wine Mixer, the real Catalina Island is a visible easter egg
   Long: The Catalina Wine Mixer wasn't filmed on Catalina. Director Adam McKay left the real island in the background as a funny easter egg.
10. Short: Dale's drum set rule came from John C. Reilly's real-life brother.
   Long: John C. Reilly says Dale's perfectionism about his drum set came from his real-life brother, who noticed every nick on his drumsticks.
11. Short: Adam Scott lip-synced 'Sweet Child O' Mine' to a hidden singer.
   Long: Adam Scott, who says he's a terrible singer, lip-synced 'Sweet Child O' Mine' to a pro singing live outside the windshield.
12. Short: The cinematographer who shot the "Bourne" trilogy also filmed "Step Brothers."
   Long: 'Bourne' trilogy cinematographer Oliver Wood filmed 'Step Brothers.' Adam McKay hired him for its hand-held camera shots.
13. Short: A full Dale and Brennan rap album was planned.
   Long: Adam McKay says they wrote 'four or five' rough raps for a Dale and Brennan album, like the movie's 'Boats N Hoes.'
14. Short: Actor John C. Reilly performed all of his own drumming in the movie.
   Long: John C. Reilly performed all of his own drumming in the movie, a skill he learned for the 1995 film 'Georgia.'
15. Short: The 'Step Brothers' DVD commentary includes songs the cast improvised.
   Long: The 'Step Brothers' DVD commentary is mostly spoken, with improvised songs by Will Ferrell, John C. Reilly and director Adam McKay.
16. Short: The original Step Brothers script was almost 1.5x as long as the final one.
   Long: The first draft was 180 pages, about 1.5x the final script, and took 8 or 9 rewrites to cut.
17. Short: Actor Richard Jenkins was bitten on the backside by a dog on set.
   Long: Actor Richard Jenkins was bitten on the backside by a dog on set. He asked director Adam McKay if he should limp, but McKay said no.
18. Short: Will Ferrell's dog-poop scene is an anecdote from his real life.
   Long: John C. Reilly says the scene of Will Ferrell licking white dog poop really happened to Ferrell.
19. Short: Will Ferrell lived at home for three years after college, like Brennan.
   Long: Will Ferrell lived at home with his mom for three years after college, just like his character Brennan.
20. Short: Adam Scott studied the Robb Report to build his character Derek.
   Long: Adam Scott bought a Robb Report to build Derek, picturing guys in suits on tarmacs in front of private jets.
